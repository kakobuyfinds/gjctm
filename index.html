How to compare spreadsheet finds

  1. Confirm the listing. Match the item ID and check the seller’s current price for your size and color.
  2. Compare measurements. Measure a garment you already own flat. Compare chest width, length and sleeve length with the listing’s size chart.
  3. Check the total cost. Add domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties before comparing two items.
  4. Review inspection photos. If your agent offers quality-control photos, compare the ordered color, size label, stitching and visible damage before approving shipment.

Questions about the ACBuy spreadsheet

Can I buy these listings through ACBuy?

The catalog links to HipobuyFind. To check whether you can order through ACBuy, open the source listing and verify that ACBuy accepts that URL and item before paying.

How do I estimate international shipping?

Use your agent’s quote for your destination, parcel dimensions and weight. Compare whether the carrier bills by actual or volumetric weight.
